# VendorShield Auditor User Guide

## Login

Start the application and open `http://127.0.0.1:8501`.

```text
Username: auditor1
Password: demo-password
```

The current project has one end-user role: Procurement Auditor.

## Day-to-day workflow

1. Open **Command Center** to review portfolio exposure, priority cases, risk distribution, and trends.
2. Use the sidebar filters to narrow the portfolio by supplier, risk level, product category, region, payment method, review status, date range, or minimum risk score.
3. Open **Transaction Review** and select a transaction requiring attention.
4. Review the transaction facts, price and timing indicators, and the plain-language reasons shown in the case file.
5. Change the review status to Pending, In Review, Escalated, Cleared, or Confirmed Issue and record an audit note.
6. Open **Supplier Intelligence** to compare supplier exposure and concentration of High-risk activity.
7. Open **New Transaction Assessment** to evaluate a transaction not already present in the historical portfolio.
8. Save a new assessment only when it should become part of the formal review queue.

## Decision-support rule

A High or Medium score means that review is recommended. The auditor must validate invoices, purchase orders, approvals, payment records, supplier documents, and procurement policy before reaching a conclusion.
